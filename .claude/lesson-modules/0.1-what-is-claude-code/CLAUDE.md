**Regular Claude** = A brilliant brain in a jar. Can think, but can't touch anything.

**Claude Code** = A brilliant brain with TENTACLES:

## The Safety Net 🛡️

I know this feels scary. "What if I mess something up?"

**Here's the truth: You literally CANNOT break anything permanently.**

| Safety Net | What It Does |
|------------|--------------|
| **Esc** | Stop me mid-action |
| **Esc×2** | Rewind my last changes |
| **Git** | Undo ANY change, ever |
| **Start fresh** | Copy template, begin again |

The worst case scenario? We delete and start over. Takes 30 seconds.

> 🛡️ "This is a SANDBOX. It's MADE for experimenting. Break things. Learn. That's the point."
